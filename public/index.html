How this registry handles data

  • De-identified at the source. Hospitals and registries strip names, phone numbers and ID numbers on their own computers. The national registry only receives a keyed pseudonym, birth month, sex and district.
  • Complements existing registries. Population cancer registries keep running; their diagnoses are linked by pseudonym so screening can be evaluated against outcomes.
  • Every flow under an agreement. Organisations send or take data only under a signed data-sharing agreement, and every access is logged.
  • Anonymised data out. This page shows suppressed totals. Approved researchers receive k-anonymous extracts only.

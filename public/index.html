How this registry protects data

🔒

De-identified at source

Hospitals remove names, phone numbers and CNICs on their own computers. The registry receives a coded ID, birth month, sex and district.

🔗

Complements existing registries

Cancer registries keep running and share diagnoses, linked by coded ID, so screening can be judged by outcomes.

📝

Every flow under agreement

Data moves only under signed agreements, and every access is logged.

📊

Anonymised data out

Public figures hide small counts. Approved researchers receive only k-anonymous extracts.
